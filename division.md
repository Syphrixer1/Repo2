# Division of Two Integers

Division is the process of finding the quotient when one integer is divided by another. Division is basically repetitive subtraction.

The division operator is `/`.

## Example

If we divide 20 by 5:

20 / 5 = 4

##To prove this with subtraction, it is 
20 - 5 = 15
15 - 5 = 10
10 - 5 = 5
5 - 5 = 0

 The number 5 was subtracted 4 times from 20, so the quotient is four.

Therefore, the quotient of 20 divided by 5 is **4**.
